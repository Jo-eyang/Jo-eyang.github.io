- Fine-tuned a **T5-small** model for Text-to-SQL generation and developed structure-preserving augmentation that jointly modifies natural-language inputs and SQL targets, expanding the training set from 4,225 to 35,777 examples.
- Achieved **81.84% Record F1** and **80.32% Record Exact Match**, improving held-out Record F1 by 7.54 points through augmentation, model selection, beam-search decoding, and execution-based evaluation.
