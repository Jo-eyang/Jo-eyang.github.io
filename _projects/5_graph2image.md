- Extended the **InstructG2I** graph-conditioned image generation framework with Graph-QFormer attention masking and CLIP-based neighborhood filtering, **reducing FID by 4.2%** by suppressing irrelevant graph context.
- Designed a **mixture-of-experts visual encoder** to replace single-path feature extraction, letting specialized experts capture complementary visual patterns and improving alignment between graph context and visual representations.
