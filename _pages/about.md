Hi! I'm Jiayi, a senior undergraduate at **[NYU Shanghai](https://shanghai.nyu.edu/)** studying Computer Science, with minors in Mathematics and Business. I spent 2025–26 studying away at NYU in New York.

I work with [Prof. Qiaoyu Tan](https://qiaoyu-tan.github.io/) on graph learning and multimodal foundation models, and with [Prof. Jinyang Li](https://jinyangli.github.io/) and Prof. Tan on LLM search agents.

My research asks how foundation models can **reason over structured, multimodal data**, and how **LLM agents can coordinate and generalize** across tasks. Recently, I have worked on:

- **Unified graph learning across modalities.** A single vision-language model that learns over text-, image-, and multimodal-attributed graphs ([OMG-VLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19128), EMNLP 2026).
- **Multi-agent coordination for graph learning.** When and how specialized LLM agents should collaborate ([GraphMAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39777), under review).
- **Generalizable search agents.** Closing the gap between general retrieval QA and long-horizon deep search.

<p class="callout">I am applying to Ph.D. programs in Computer Science for <strong>Fall 2027</strong>.</p>
